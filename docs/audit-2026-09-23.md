# Play Pot audit, 23 September 2026

Read-only audit of Play Pot at commit `c8fca3f`, done with code review plus a
375×812 phone preview of the local dev server. Findings marked "verified" were
reproduced in that preview.

Fixes below were deployed on 23 September 2026 as Worker version
`75a0d5c9-9c14-40d1-9fa8-efce7d521cc8`.

**Status:** Fixed · Partly fixed · Open · Accepted (known and kept on purpose)

## Top 5 recommendations

1. **Guard confirmations against double-taps.** Fixed in `c9fe42e`.
2. **Keep UNDO visible after OUT.** Fixed in `1c6b6ed`.
3. **Keep earlier-day families out of NEXT DUE.** Fixed in `55675ea`.
4. **Let the tracker open offline.** Open; service-worker caching is ruled out for now.
5. **Make OUT reachable without scrolling.** Fixed in `7317041`.
